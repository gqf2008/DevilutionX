## Testing Strategy

### Unit Tests

1. **Light Table Tests**
   - Verify black (0) stays black
   - Verify white (255) goes to black at max darkness
   - Verify Hell blood wall special case
   - Verify Nest/Crypt lava special case

2. **Falloff Tests**
   - Center is always 0 (brightest)
   - Edge is always 15 (darkest) at max radius
   - Intermediate values follow curve

3. **Interpolation Tests**
   - (0,0) offset matches non-interpolated
   - Symmetric around center

### Integration Tests

1. Add light at position, verify buffer values
2. Move light, verify old position restored
3. Multiple overlapping lights, verify min behavior
